# Synthetic correction and approval separation

The protected synthetic review path records the server-derived issuer-and-subject identity hash of the person who starts a new review. It rejects approval by that identity or by an identity that made any correction in that review, even if the identity later receives the Reviewer role. A browser-supplied actor is not trusted. A different provisioned Reviewer can approve after the existing authorization, source, revision and financial checks pass.

This is a narrow synthetic safeguard, not proof of separation between people. One person may hold different accounts or identities. Historical correction actors from other systems might not be comparable. The uploader is not recorded: if one identity uploads and another starts the review, this check does not identify the uploader. Existing reviews without a recorded preparer remain replayable and use the earlier correction-author check; their review starters cannot be inferred. Existing reviews are not changed or migrated. An organization needs a trusted identity and provenance model, policy for historic records, and independent review before claiming complete separation of duties.
